What happens when I click Connect?

Clicking Connect opens the sign-in page of Google (for Blogger) or Pinterest in a new tab. Sign in, check what BlogNeo can access, and approve. You come straight back to BlogNeo, and your connection shows as Connected. You can disconnect anytime from this page — BlogNeo never sees your password.
